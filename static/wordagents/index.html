    How to play

    1. Split into red and blue teams. Each picks one spymaster; everyone else is an operative.
    2. Spymasters see the key to all 25 words. Give one word and a number linking that many of your team’s agents.
    3. Operatives discuss, then reveal one card at a time. You may make the clue number plus one guesses, or end your turn early.
    4. Your agent: keep going. A civilian or opposing agent: your turn ends. The assassin: your team immediately loses.
    5. Find all your team’s agents first to win. The starting team has nine agents; the other has eight.

    Clues must be about meaning, not spelling, position or gestures. Don’t use a word still visible on the board. Agree on borderline clues with the other spymaster.
