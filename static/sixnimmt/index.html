    How to play

    Everyone starts each round with ten cards. Choose one card and lock it in. When everyone is ready, the lowest card plays first. Each card goes after the closest lower number at the end of a row.

    If your card is lower than all four row ends, you choose a row to take. If your card is sixth in its row, you take the five cards already there. Your card starts the new row either way.

    The number next to the little bull is the card’s penalty. Finish the round when everyone runs out of cards. Once anyone reaches 66 penalty points at the end of a round, the lowest total wins.
